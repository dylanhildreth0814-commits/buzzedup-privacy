03

Eligibility

The Sweepstakes is open only to legal residents of the United States who currently reside in the State of Ohio and are 21 years of age or older at the time of entry.

The following individuals are not eligible to enter or win:

  • Employees, officers, members, and contractors of BuzzedUp LLC
  • Employees, owners, and managers of any venue participating in BuzzedUp
  • The immediate family members (spouse, parent, child, or sibling) of any of the above
  • Any person living in the same household as any of the above, whether related or not

You must have a BuzzedUp account with an email address on file in order to redeem points for entries. This is required so that we are able to contact you if you win. Entries submitted through the free online entry form must include a valid email address.

Eligibility is self-certified. Sponsor does not verify age or residency at the time of entry and relies on the entrant's confirmation of eligibility at the time a prize is claimed. Sponsor reserves the right to verify the eligibility of any entrant or winner at any time and to disqualify any entry that does not meet these requirements.

04

Entry Periods

The Sweepstakes is conducted in consecutive two-week entry periods (each, an "Entry Period"). Each Entry Period begins at 12:00:00 a.m. Eastern Time on a Monday and ends at 11:59:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the second following Sunday.

The start date, end date, and prize for the current Entry Period are displayed in the BuzzedUp app. Sponsor's computer is the official timekeeping device for the Sweepstakes.

Sponsor reserves the right to modify the schedule of, suspend, or discontinue future Entry Periods at any time.

05

How to Enter

There are two ways to enter. Both methods are free. Entries received through either method have an equal chance of winning.

Method 1 — Redeem BuzzedUp points. BuzzedUp users earn points for using the app. Points may be earned by rating a deal, voting in a deal poll, referring a friend, checking in at a participating venue, or tagging a vibe. Points cannot be purchased, have no cash value, and are not transferable. Within the BuzzedUp app, you may redeem 25 points for one (1) entry into the current Entry Period.

Method 2 — Free online entry form. You may enter without earning or redeeming any points by completing the free online entry form at the BuzzedUp free entry form. No app download, no purchase, and no points are required to use this method.

Method 3 — Welcome entry. Each BuzzedUp user with an email address on their account may claim one (1) free entry, one time only, into the first Entry Period in which they claim it. No points, purchase, or other action is required. This entry is available once per person for the lifetime of the account and counts toward the entry limit below.

Limit one (1) online entry form submission per person per calendar day. Multiple submissions by the same person on the same calendar day will be counted as a single entry. Submitting the form more than once in a day will not increase your chances of winning.

Entry limit. Regardless of the method or combination of methods used, each person may receive a maximum of ten (10) entries per Entry Period. Entries in excess of this limit will be void.

Entries become the property of Sponsor and will not be acknowledged or returned. Entries that are incomplete, illegible, or submitted by any method other than those described above are void. Any attempt to obtain more than the stated number of entries by using multiple accounts, multiple email addresses, or any automated or fraudulent means will void all of that person's entries.

06

Drawing and Odds

Within one (1) business day after the close of each Entry Period, Sponsor will select one (1) potential winner at random from among all eligible entries received during that Entry Period.

Each entry is a separate chance to win. A person holding five (5) entries has five (5) chances to win. Odds of winning depend on the total number of eligible entries received during the Entry Period.

Sponsor's selection is final. Sponsor reserves the right to select an alternate winner if a selected entry is determined to be ineligible, fraudulent, or in violation of these Official Rules.

07

Winner Notification and Claiming a Prize

The potential winner will be notified by email at the address associated with their BuzzedUp account or, for free online entry form entrants, at the email address provided on the form.

To claim the prize, the potential winner must reply to the notification email within seven (7) calendar days confirming eligibility. A reply stating "I confirm I am 21 years of age or older and currently reside in Ohio" is sufficient.

If a potential winner does not respond within seven (7) calendar days, cannot be contacted, declines the prize, is found to be ineligible, or fails to confirm eligibility as described above, the prize will be forfeited and Sponsor may select an alternate winner from among the remaining eligible entries.

Sponsor may require additional proof of eligibility before awarding a prize. Sponsor is not responsible for email that is undeliverable, filtered as spam, or otherwise not received.

08

The prize for each Entry Period is one (1) gift card. The specific prize for the current Entry Period is displayed in the BuzzedUp app.

The approximate retail value ("ARV") of the prize for any Entry Period will not exceed two hundred fifty U.S. dollars ($250.00). Only one (1) prize will be awarded per Entry Period.

Prizes are not transferable and no substitution or cash equivalent is available, except that Sponsor reserves the right to substitute a prize of equal or greater value if the advertised prize becomes unavailable.

All federal, state, and local taxes on any prize are the sole responsibility of the winner. Any costs associated with redeeming or using a prize that are not expressly stated as included are the responsibility of the winner.

Prizes are subject to the terms and conditions of the issuing merchant. Sponsor is not responsible for a gift card issuer's refusal to honor a gift card, or for a venue closing or ceasing operations.
